I’m excited to share a healthcare analytics portfolio project as I continue exploring internship opportunities in health informatics, healthcare data analytics, and healthcare IT for 2027.

My project, Healthcare Access & Scheduling Analytics, uses 1,200 synthetic appointment records to explore appointment no-show rates, check-in wait times, and how scheduling lead times may relate to missed visits. Using SQL and Python, I developed reproducible queries and visualizations to examine these patterns and consider how data can inform hospital operations.

One lesson that stands out is that meaningful healthcare analytics requires more than building a dashboard. It requires clear definitions, reliable data, awareness of patient privacy, and thoughtful interpretation before making operational decisions.
